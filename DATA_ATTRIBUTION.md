# Data attribution

СПОТ uses open place and map data.

## Places

The city catalog is seeded from the **Overture Maps Foundation Places** theme.
Overture Places is distributed under permissive licenses that vary by upstream source, including CDLA Permissive 2.0, Apache 2.0, and CC0 1.0.

Source and license details: https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/

The Overture Places theme contains no OpenStreetMap data. When СПОТ later adds OpenStreetMap-derived place enrichment, those records must retain the Open Database License (ODbL) attribution and share-alike obligations applicable to the derived database.

## Base map

Map tiles currently shown in the Telegram Mini App are based on OpenStreetMap data.
